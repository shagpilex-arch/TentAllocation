Support Bear Camp

Completely optional, gently appreciated, and absolutely not required to use anything here.

A small Scout honesty-box request

If a tool has been useful, please consider the £1 honesty box.

Take a moment at the virtual camp gate. Bear Camp is offered on trust: no paywall, no lock, and no awkward begging bowl. Just a simple request that if something here helps your camp, you consider buying me a coffee for £1.

The Scout laws that feel especially relevant here are:

  • A Scout is to be trusted
  • A Scout is helpful
  • A Scout is friendly and considerate
  • A Scout makes good use of time and is careful of possessions and property

These tools were built in my own time to help leaders spend less time wrestling with spreadsheets and more time planning proper camps, sorting kit, supporting young people, and doing all the other unpaid behind-the-scenes jobs that somehow appear five minutes before departure.

The tools are available either way. This is just a campfire-sized thank you.
