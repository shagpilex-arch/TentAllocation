A small honesty-box request

If a planner has been useful, please consider the £1 honesty box.

Bear Camp planners are offered on trust: no paywall, no lock, and no awkward begging bowl. Just a simple request that if one of the planners helps your group, you consider buying me a coffee for £1.

Scout Camp Planner and Camp Planner for Guides were built in my own time to help leaders spend less time wrestling with admin and more time planning proper camps, sorting kit, supporting young people, and doing the other unpaid behind-the-scenes jobs that appear before departure.
