A practical campfire for small projects

Bear Camp is where I keep the tools I build in personal time: camp planning apps, tiny utilities, ESP32 experiments, printer files, and the odd idea that got far enough to deserve a page.

These are hobby projects, not polished corporate software products. They may change, improve, get replaced, or never be "finished" in the traditional sense. The aim is simpler: make useful things, explain them honestly, and share them without tracking, cookies, analytics, or fuss.

Camp notes

  • Mostly built for real planning problems.
  • Provided as-is, with plain explanations.
  • No popups, tracking, cookies, or paid services.
  • Camp Tent Allocation is the main available project right now.

A small Scout honesty-box request

If Bear Camp saves you an evening, £1 is a fair campfire-sized thank you.

This site is offered on trust. There is no paywall, no lock, and no awkward begging bowl. Just a simple honesty-box request: if a Bear Camp tool helps your camp, please consider buying me a coffee for £1.

The Scout laws that feel especially relevant here are:

  • A Scout is to be trusted
  • A Scout is helpful
  • A Scout is friendly and considerate
  • A Scout makes good use of time and is careful of possessions and property

The projects are still available either way. But if one saves your group even one evening of admin, £1 in the honesty box helps recognise the work behind it and keeps future improvements possible.
