Two practical camp planners: one for Scout camps, and one for Guide camps. Both help with people, sleeping plans, rotas, menus, kit, daily plans, exports, and online access.

Camp admin, kept together

Bear Camp focuses on the main planning jobs that leaders need before and during camp, without splitting everything across several spreadsheets.

Choose the Scout planner or the Guide planner, then use the Windows app or the online version depending on how your team prefers to work.
