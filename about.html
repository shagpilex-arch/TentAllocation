About Bear Camp

A small, practical place for camp planning tools that started as "I could probably make that" and became useful enough to share.

What it is

Bear Camp is the home for Scout Camp Planner and Camp Planner for Guides.

Both planners are built for real camp admin: people, sleeping arrangements, rota jobs, menus, kit, shopping, daily plans, exports, and online access.

How the projects work

These are hobby-built tools, so they do not pretend to be corporate software with a formal support contract. They are released because they solve real camp planning jobs and are useful enough to share.

The Microsoft Store listings are the official Windows download source. The online versions are available from the project pages and work on mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop, and Mac.

What is here now

  • Scout Camp Planner: the green Scout-focused camp planning app.
  • Camp Planner for Guides: the blue Guide-focused camp planning app.

The general promise

Bear Camp should stay plain and useful: no tracking, no cookies, no analytics, no fake downloads, and no pretending a hobby project is a giant software company.
