About Bear Camp

A small, practical place for tools that started as "I could probably make that" and became useful enough to share.

What it is

Bear Camp is the home for Camp Planner and other practical camp planning tools.

The feel is meant to be Scout camp, practical admin, and friendly hobby software in one place. Camp Planner is available for Windows from the Microsoft Store, and the older standalone tool pages remain available.

How the projects work

These are hobby-built tools, so they do not pretend to be corporate software with a formal support contract. They are released because they solve real camp planning jobs and are useful enough to share.

They may be updated, changed, or improved over time. The Microsoft Store listings are the official download source.

What is here now

  • Camp Planner: the new combined program for tents, chores, menus, kit, schedules, people, and exports.
  • Camp Tent Allocation: the original visual tool for planning tent groups, now included in Camp Planner.
  • Camp Chores: the original rota planning tool for camp duties, now included in Camp Planner.

The general promise

Bear Camp should stay plain and useful: no tracking, no cookies, no analytics, no fake downloads, and no pretending a hobby project is a giant software company in a fleece.
