About Bear Camp

A small, practical place for tools that started as "I could probably make that" and became useful enough to share.

What it is

Bear Camp is the home for my hobby projects: camp tools, small utilities, ESP32 experiments, 3D printer files, and anything else that fits the useful-little-projects shelf.

It is mostly branded as Bear Camp because the site is about the projects, not a big personal profile. The feel is meant to be Scout camp, woodland tech shed, and friendly hobby software in one place.

How the projects work

These are hobby projects, so they do not follow a neat corporate product roadmap. Some are ready to use, some are nearly there, and some are placeholders for ideas that need more time.

Projects may be updated, changed, replaced, or retired over time. They may also never be "finished" in the traditional sense, because the real goal is to solve a practical problem well enough and keep improving when it makes sense.

What is here now

  • Camp Tent Allocation: the main available project, built for planning tent groups at Scout camps and similar group events.
  • Camp Chore Planner: a rota planning tool for camp duties, currently in development.
  • Foot Pedal: an Olympus RS24 foot pedal mapper for configurable keyboard macros, available on request.
  • BenMail: a simple Windows mail app project using MailKit and MimeKit, available on request.
  • C3 Emulator: an ESP32-C3 Super Mini emulator shell for GPIO, peripheral, and serial experiments, available on request.
  • 3D Printer Files: a future catalogue of free STL downloads.

The general promise

Bear Camp should stay plain and useful: no tracking, no cookies, no analytics, no fake downloads, and no pretending a hobby project is a giant software company in a fleece.
