Camp Planner for Guides

A Guide-focused Bear Camp planner for unit camps, covering people, sleeping plans, rota jobs, menus, kit, shopping, schedules, exports, and online collaboration.

Guide edition

Overview

Camp Planner for Guides keeps the same practical planning workflow as the Scout version, but with Guide-focused wording, blue visual styling, and screenshots that match the Guide edition.

It is built for Guide camps where leaders need one place for people, sleeping arrangements, rota jobs, shopping, menus, kit, daily plans, and useful paperwork.

What it includes

  • People and teams Keep names, teams, dietary notes, medical notes, and camp details together.
  • Sleeping plan Arrange tents, bunk rooms, fields, and who sleeps where.
  • Rota jobs Plan jobs across morning, afternoon, evening, and custom sessions.
  • Menus Plan meals, pudding, dietary notes, and day-by-day menu checks.
  • Kit and shopping Track group kit, participant kit, buying lists, and camp shopping.
  • The Plan Build a daily timeline for activities, timings, groups, tents, or individuals.
  • Exports Produce camp packs, PDFs, printable outputs, and CSV folders.
  • Online version Work from a phone, tablet, laptop, desktop, or Mac.
