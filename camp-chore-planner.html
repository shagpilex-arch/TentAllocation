Camp Chore Planner

A nearly-done rota planning tool for camp chores, patrol duties, free time, and all the jobs that keep a camp moving.

In development

Overview

Camp Chore Planner is being built for planning camp chore rotas, especially for Scout camps. The idea is to make duty planning easier to understand than a sprawling rota spreadsheet.

It is nearly done, but this page is intentionally still a placeholder until the download is ready.

Possible uses

  • Patrol chore rota Share jobs fairly across patrols or groups.
  • Free time rota Plan who is available and when.
  • Washing up Make the post-meal rota less arguable.
  • Food prep Allocate kitchen help across the camp.
  • Cleaning Keep cleaning jobs visible and scheduled.
  • Wood collecting Add outdoor duties without losing them in the margins.
  • Tent inspection prep Plan inspection prep and tidy-up time.
  • Multiple days Spread camp duties across longer events.

Current status

In development. The core idea is close, but the release is not ready yet.

No download is provided until there is a clearly intended release.
