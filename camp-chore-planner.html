Camp Chores

A rota planning tool for camp chores, patrol duties, free time, and all the jobs that keep a camp moving.

Available now

Overview

Camp Chores is built for planning camp chore rotas, especially for Scout camps. It makes duty planning easier to understand than a sprawling rota spreadsheet.

Add people, groups, rota items, and sessions, then build a clear schedule for the work that needs doing across camp.

What it is for

  • Patrol chore rota Share jobs fairly across patrols or groups.
  • Free time rota Plan who is available and when.
  • Washing up Make the post-meal rota less arguable.
  • Food prep Allocate kitchen help across the camp.
  • Cleaning Keep cleaning jobs visible and scheduled.
  • Wood collecting Add outdoor duties without losing them in the margins.
  • Tent inspection prep Plan inspection prep and tidy-up time.
  • Multiple days Spread camp duties across longer events.

Current status

Available now. Camp Chores remains available from the Microsoft Store.

The chore rota features from this standalone tool are now also included in Camp Planner, the new combined Bear Camp program.
