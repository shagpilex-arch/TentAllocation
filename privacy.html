Privacy Policy

Camp Tent Allocation

Simple summary: Camp Tent Allocation is a local desktop app. It does not collect, sell, share, upload, or transmit your camp data to the developer or to any external server.

1. About this app

Camp Tent Allocation is a desktop application designed to help users plan tent allocations for camps and similar events. The app allows users to create, open, save, and manage camp allocation files on their own device.

2. Information entered into the app

Users may choose to enter information such as camper names, tent names, tent types, and allocation details. This information is used only within the app so that the user can organise and manage tent allocations.

The app does not require users to create an online account, sign in, or provide information directly to the developer.

3. Data collection

Camp Tent Allocation does not collect personal information from users.

The app does not:

  • send camp data to the developer;
  • upload camper names or allocation details to an external server;
  • sell, rent, or share user data;
  • track users across other apps or websites;
  • use advertising or marketing trackers;
  • collect analytics about individual users;
  • access contacts, messages, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

4. Local files

Camp Tent Allocation works with files that the user chooses to create, open, save, or export. These files remain under the user’s control on their own device or chosen storage location.

If a user chooses to save files in a cloud-synced folder, such as OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or another service, that storage is controlled by the user and by the relevant cloud provider. Camp Tent Allocation itself does not upload those files.

5. Internet access

Camp Tent Allocation does not require an online account and does not send user-entered camp data to an online service.

The app may link to a support website or information page, but opening such links is controlled by the user.

6. Children’s information

The app may be used by adults to organise camp information that could include children’s names. This information is entered and managed by the user locally.

The developer does not receive, collect, store, or process children’s information through the app.

7. Restricted capabilities

The app may be packaged as a desktop application and may use the Windows runFullTrust capability. This is used only to allow the packaged desktop application to run normally.

This capability is not used to modify system settings, install services, monitor users, access other apps’ data, or perform administrative actions.

8. Data security

Because camp allocation data is stored locally by the user, users are responsible for keeping their own files secure. Users should take care when storing, sharing, backing up, or printing files that contain personal information.

9. Data deletion

Camp Tent Allocation does not hold a central copy of user data. To delete data created with the app, users can delete the relevant saved files from their own device or storage location.

10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time if the app changes or if further clarification is needed. The latest version will be made available on this page.
