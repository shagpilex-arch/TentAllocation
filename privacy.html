Privacy Policy

This policy covers Camp Tent Allocation and Camp Chores.

Simple summary

Camp Tent Allocation and Camp Chores are local desktop apps. They do not collect, sell, share, upload, or transmit your camp data to the developer or to any external server.

1. About these apps

Camp Tent Allocation helps users plan tent allocations for camps and similar events. Camp Chores helps users plan camp chore rotas, patrol duties, sessions, and related camp jobs.

Both apps allow users to create, open, save, and manage their own planning files on their own device.

2. Information entered into the apps

Users may choose to enter information such as camper names, tent names, group names, rota items, duties, sessions, notes, and allocation details. This information is used only within the app so the user can organise and manage their camp planning.

The apps do not require users to create an online account, sign in, or provide information directly to the developer.

3. Data collection

Camp Tent Allocation and Camp Chores do not collect personal information from users.

The apps do not:

  • send camp data to the developer;
  • upload camper names, rota details, or allocation details to an external server;
  • sell, rent, or share user data;
  • track users across other apps or websites;
  • use advertising or marketing trackers;
  • collect analytics about individual users;
  • access contacts, messages, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

4. Local files

The apps work with files that the user chooses to create, open, save, or export. These files remain under the user’s control on their own device or chosen storage location.

If a user chooses to save files in a cloud-synced folder, such as OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or another service, that storage is controlled by the user and by the relevant cloud provider. The apps themselves do not upload those files.

5. Internet access

The apps do not require an online account and do not send user-entered camp data to an online service.

The apps may link to a support website, privacy policy, or information page, but opening such links is controlled by the user.

6. Children’s information

The apps may be used by adults to organise camp information that could include children’s names. This information is entered and managed by the user locally.

The developer does not receive, collect, store, or process children’s information through the apps.

7. Restricted capabilities

The apps may be packaged as desktop applications and may use the Windows runFullTrust capability. This is used only to allow the packaged desktop applications to run normally.

This capability is not used to modify system settings, install services, monitor users, access other apps’ data, or perform administrative actions.

8. Data security

Because camp planning data is stored locally by the user, users are responsible for keeping their own files secure. Users should take care when storing, sharing, backing up, or printing files that contain personal information.

9. Data deletion

The apps do not hold a central copy of user data. To delete data created with the apps, users can delete the relevant saved files from their own device or storage location.

10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time if the apps change or if further clarification is needed. The latest version will be made available on this page.

11. Contact

For support or privacy questions, please contact Website@bearcamp.co.uk.
